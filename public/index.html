IF YOU NEED HELP RIGHT NOW

Urgent support should never wait for an app.

Anon is not monitored and cannot provide crisis intervention. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department now. If possible, stay with a trusted person. Do not wait for a reply from this website.

NO SURPRISES

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Before you take a step, you deserve to understand what this platform does, what it doesn't do and how to protect your information.

Is Anon completely anonymous?

No digital service can guarantee anonymity. This prototype asks for very little information, but the hosting provider and normal internet infrastructure may process technical data. Avoid entering identifying or sensitive information.

Can I speak to a counsellor or book therapy here?

Not at this stage. Anon is a counselling access concept and working software prototype. It does not currently provide live counsellor matching, appointments, therapy, diagnosis, video sessions or clinical follow-up.

Are the 17 support pathways available services?

They are the 17 topic areas in the supplied architecture. They describe the intended scope; they do not confirm that practitioners, partners, appointments or treatment are available.

What happens when I submit the demo form?

The browser sends the selected topic, optional display name and consent flag to the prototype API. The backend records a demonstration entry locally. It is not sent to a counsellor. Please use fictional, non-sensitive information only.

Does Anon provide emergency or crisis support?

No. The website is not monitored and cannot assess risk or dispatch help. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

What is needed before a real launch?

Clinical governance, verified practitioners, safeguarding and crisis protocols, Botswana privacy and legal review, secure production data handling, tested access controls, a retention policy and operational support must be established before real users submit sensitive information.
