SMALL, PRACTICAL TOOLS

A moment to pause.

5–4–3–2–1 grounding

Notice five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell and one you taste. Stop if this increases distress.

Gentle breathing

If comfortable, breathe naturally and gently. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.

Reach out

Consider contacting a trusted person and sharing what kind of support would help right now.

DEMONSTRATION ONLY

Explore a next step.

This form records a local demo request only. It does not connect you to a counsellor. Do not enter your real name, contact details or health information.

Need urgent help?

This app is not monitored and cannot provide crisis intervention. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department now. If possible, stay with a trusted person.

TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Your privacy and safety.

Is Anon anonymous?

This prototype asks for very little information, but no digital service can guarantee anonymity. Do not enter identifying or sensitive information.

Does this provide therapy?

No. It is an access concept and demonstration only. No live counsellor matching, therapy, diagnosis, video sessions or crisis monitoring is available.

Are all listed services available now?

No. The list reflects the source architecture and intended service areas, not confirmed staffing, partnerships or service availability.
