Publication, data quality and privacy safeguards

01 / PUBLICATION BOUNDARY

Respect GABS's public edition

The GABS public profile excludes detailed numerical impact tables. Anon withholds detailed figures until written publication permission is provided.

02 / SOURCE CONTROL

Use approved sources

Every future release must identify the approved report, reporting period, metric definitions, responsible owner and approval date.

03 / AGGREGATION

Avoid misleading totals

Do not combine activity reach values or present activity contacts as unique people unless GABS supplies a validated deduplication method and approves the definition.

04 / DATA MINIMISATION

Keep private details private

Do not publish names, contact details, HIV status, diagnoses, counselling notes, beneficiary identifiers or safeguarding disclosures.

05 / DISCLOSURE REVIEW

Suppress risky detail

GABS must review re-identification risks, small groups, sensitive breakdowns and public release conditions before any dataset is published.

06 / GOVERNANCE

Keep accountability

Use the aggregate update template and governance procedure in the repository. This page is not an administrator portal or beneficiary register.

What is required before figures can be added?

Written GABS authorisation for public publication, an approved dated aggregate dataset, metric definitions, a reporting-period cut-off, a named programme data owner and a reviewer. No personal or case-level data should be supplied to Anon for this public dashboard.
